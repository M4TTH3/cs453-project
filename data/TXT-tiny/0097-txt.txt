On 20 Sep 2016, at 11:33, Graham White <graham.white@shropshire.gov.uk> wrote:
Dear Councillor Mrs Barrow,
 
I am writing to tell you that an allegation that you have failed, 
or may have failed, to comply with the Code of Conduct of 
Shropshire Council has been received from Councillor Dave 
Tremellen.
                                                                    
It is alleged as follows:
 
“As Local Member for the St.Oswalds Division of 
Shropshire Council, Councillor Barrow compromised 
her position as a member of the North Planning 
Committee by failing to report breaches of planning law 
on the part of the British Ironwork Centre, Oswestry.
 
As a visitor to the site and a personal friend of Mr Clive 
Knowles, the owner of the British Ironwork Centre, 
Councillor Barrow will have been aware of 
developments at the British Ironwork Centre and, as a 
member of the North Planning Committee, will have 
known whether those developments were subject to 
Planning requirements and been aware of the absence 
of any application(s) pertaining to them.
 
By failing to advise Shropshire Council Planning 
department of these breaches of planning law, and 
failing to advise Mr Clive Knowles of his 
contravention(s) knowing him to be in breach, as 
witness Mr Knowles' admission of his deliberately 
contravening planning law, Councillor Barrow 
compromised both her own integrity as an elected 
member of a public body and encouraged Mr Knowles 
in the pursuit of his breach.”
                                                                                  
Any comments that you wish to make in relation to the 
allegation must be made in writing as soon as possible and 
will be taken into account when consideration is given to 
what, if any, further action is appropriate. At this stage all 
that will happen is that the allegation will be considered 
based solely on the information provided by you and the 
complainant along with any relevant information readily 
available such as minutes of Council meetings. 
 
Once a decision has been made you and the complainant 
will be told about the outcome and what will happen next.
 
At this stage you are advised not to contact anyone else as 
this could compromise you or the fairness of the process.

this. You should also be aware that any written request for
