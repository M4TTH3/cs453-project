Due Regard for Permanent/Experimental TROs

The Council has a duty to give ‘due regard to’ the following three aims of the public sector equality duty (section 149 of the Equality Act 2010) during the development of the ‘policy’. 

Eliminate discrimination, harassment and victimisation and any other conduct prohibited by the ACT:
Advance equality of opportunity between people who share a protected characteristic and people who do not share it; and
Foster good relations between people who share a protected characteristic
